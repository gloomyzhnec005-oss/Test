## Post 6 — “First steps” guide

```
🧭 First steps in Mystic Horizon

1️⃣ Follow the story: the “Story” tab in Quests; the golden arrow near your hero shows the way.
2️⃣ Fight in dungeons of your level — that’s the fastest way to level, gold and gear.
3️⃣ Wear your best items and put runes into your weapon.
4️⃣ Upgrade skills (hero window → “Skills”) and stats in the Hall of Masters.
5️⃣ Claim the login reward every day — day 7 is the biggest.
6️⃣ Don’t farm bosses far below your level: they give no loot, look for dungeons of your level.

📚 All the details — “Game” menu → “Encyclopedia”.
```
